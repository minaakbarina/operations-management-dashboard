# Operations Management Dashboard

## Professional Portfolio

This project demonstrates practical capabilities in **Operations Management, Customer Service, KPI Monitoring, Team Performance, Reporting, Issue Management, and Continuous Improvement**.

The dashboard uses simulated operational data to demonstrate how performance information can be monitored, analyzed, and translated into operational actions.

---

## 1. Business Objective

The objective of this dashboard is to support an operations manager in:

- Monitoring service performance
- Tracking team productivity
- Identifying operational issues
- Monitoring customer service KPIs
- Managing escalations
- Planning corrective actions
- Supporting continuous improvement

---

## 2. Operational Management Framework

The management approach follows this cycle:

```text
Data Collection
      ↓
KPI Monitoring
      ↓
Performance Analysis
      ↓
Issue Identification
      ↓
Root Cause Analysis
      ↓
Action Planning
      ↓
Performance Review
      ↓
Continuous Improvement

```
